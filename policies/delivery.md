# In-transit orders

An in-transit order is not treated as lost until the promised delivery date has passed by five calendar days. Ask the customer to check the tracking link while the order remains inside this window.

# Missing deliveries

After the five-day grace period, route the case to a delivery investigation. Do not promise a replacement or refund before the investigation completes.
